STAPLE & FANCY - BALLARD, SEATTLE

4739 Ballard Ave NW, Seattle, WA 98107

Staple & Fancy, located in Seattle’s vibrant Ballard neighborhood, serves exquisite Italian-inspired cuisine with a modern twist. Our chef-curated multi-course feasts showcase seasonal ingredients and innovative flavors. Enjoy expertly crafted craft cocktails, a carefully selected wine list featuring both old and new world wines, and the exceptional, intimate service that defines us. Join us for a memorable meal that combines sophistication with comfort while we handle every detail of your dining experience.

Vegan, gluten-free penne pasta is available for substitution on most pasta dishes.
