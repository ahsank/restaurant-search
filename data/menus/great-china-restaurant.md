# Great China Restaurant

Address: 13501 100th Ave NE, Kirkland, WA 98034
City: Kirkland, WA
Cuisine: chinese, chinese-american
Price: $$
Source: manual-pdf
Description: Great China Restaurant has been proudly serving the Kirkland area fresh Chinese-American food for three generations. All dishes are made from scratch with fresh ingredients delivered daily, including spring rolls, sauces, and hot mustard. Menu spans soups, noodle soups, chow mein, fried rice, poultry, beef, pork, tofu, and seafood with many spicy options.
